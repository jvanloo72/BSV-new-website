Aaron Belcher is a corporate and M&A lawyer, who routinely advises clients in
the technology and life sciences sectors in complex M&A transactions. Aaron's
clients have included both public and private acquirers, private equity firms,
and venture-backed target companies and their investors.

Aaron's practice has included both domestic and cross-border acquisitions,
investments, asset purchases, securities, and corporate governance advice.

Prior to Belcher, Smolen & Van Loo, Aaron practiced for several years at Dewey &
LeBoeuf LLP's New York and Palo Alto offices, providing corporate and M&A legal
services to technology and life science clients.

Aaron obtained his B.A. from the University of Michigan and his J.D. from NYU
Law School, and is admitted to practice in both California and New York.
