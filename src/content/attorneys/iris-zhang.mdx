Iris Zhang's practice focuses on securities law, regulatory matters, financial
reporting, and compliance.

Iris received her J.D. from Columbia Law School, where she earned the Parker
School Recognition of Achievement in International and Comparative Law and served
on the staff of the Columbia Journal of Tax Law. She holds an M.S. in Management
and Strategy from the London School of Economics, a B.Econ. from Nanjing
University, and a Certificate in Global Business Law and Governance.

Before joining the firm, Iris was an associate in the audit practice of
PricewaterhouseCoopers, focusing on financial reporting and compliance, and a
legal intern at the New Jersey Bureau of Securities, working on securities
registration and enforcement. She is fluent in English and Mandarin Chinese.
