Jon Van Loo brings over 15 years' experience providing tax advice to clients
facing complex tax situations. At BSV, Jon advises clients on the tax aspects of
M&A transactions, including qualifying for tax-free reorganizations, asset and
stock sales, and the capital gains exemption for qualified small business stock.

Since establishing his own tax practice in 2017, Jon has been actively involved
in advising crypto funds and blockchain projects on a variety of tax matters and
has continued to advise some of the same original clients from that time. Over
the years, Jon has advised crypto clients on matters ranging from the taxation
of ICOs and token sales, cryptocurrency compensation, the creation and sale of
new crypto products, and the taxation of a variety of cross-border crypto
transactions.

Having begun his career as a U.S. tax advisor in London, Jon has deep experience
advising on international and cross-border tax matters. He continues to advise
clients on a variety of international tax matters, including individuals
expatriating from the U.S. and foreign corporations entering the United States.
Jon regularly collaborates with other lawyers and law firms as outside tax
counsel on complex tax matters.

Jon began his career at the global law firm Linklaters and practiced for several
years at the New York office of Dechert.

## Representative experience

- Representing numerous founders and companies in tax-free and taxable sales of
  their companies to buyers for cash and stock
- Tax-efficient structuring of cryptocurrency and other consideration in
  transactions
- Advising on the tax aspects of various cryptocurrency transactions, including
  the use of cryptocurrency as compensation

Jon is a recognized thought leader on tax issues for cryptocurrency, having
spoken at PLI and other venues and published articles on the subject.
