Belcher, Smolen & Van Loo LLP represented DesignMind, a San Francisco-based data and analytics services provider, in its sale to 3Cloud, which closed on March 3, 2025. The acquisition of DesignMind greatly expands 3Cloud's Data & AI practice. With DesignMind's team of skilled engineers, 3Cloud now surpasses 1,000 Azure experts and remains the largest pure-play Microsoft Azure services firm. This acquisition furthers 3Cloud's mission to transform clients into AI-enabled enterprises leveraging Microsoft technology. We are proud to have guided DesignMind through this strategic transaction and further details on the transaction and the significance of the acquisition [can be found here](https://3cloudsolutions.com/resources/3cloud-acquires-designmind/).
