Belcher, Smolen & Van Loo LLP represented Sano Intelligence in its agreement to sell its assets to One Drop. One Drop, a leader in digital care solutions for people living with diabetes and other chronic conditions, acquired all of the assets and intellectual property of Sano Intelligence, an innovator in health sensing technology.

Sano's continuous glucose sensing platform will be integrated into a comprehensive multi-sensorial solution from One Drop. The planned development marks a significant milestone in One Drop's effort to bring personalized, adaptive, integrated health solutions to people in real time via their mobile phones. Further details on the transaction and the significance of the acquisition for One Drop's solutions [can be found here](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/one-drop-acquires-sano-301038963.html).
