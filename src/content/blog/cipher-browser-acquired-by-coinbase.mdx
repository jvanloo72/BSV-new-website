Belcher, Smolen & Van Loo LLP represented Cipher Browser in its acquisition by Coinbase, as reported by Coinbase on April 13. Cipher Browser is a decentralized app browser and wallet for the Ethereum blockchain, while Coinbase is a leading cryptocurrency exchange. Following the acquisition, Coinbase will integrate Cipher Browser into its own decentralized mobile browser. Further details on the acquisition [can be found here](https://techcrunch.com/2018/04/13/coinbase-acquires-decentralized-app-browser-wallet-cipher-browser/).
