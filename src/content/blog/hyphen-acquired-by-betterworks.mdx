BSV represented Hyphen, a leading employee listening and engagement platform based in San Francisco, in its sale to Betterworks. Betterworks will integrate Hyphen and its products in the Betterworks software solution for enterprises to have the capability to generate actionable insights from their workforce and create goals to drive measurable improvements. Further details on the transaction can be found [here](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/betterworks-acquires-hyphen-enabling-every-enterprise-to-better-connect-employee-engagement-to-business-impact-301008194.html).
