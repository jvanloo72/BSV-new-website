Belcher, Smolen & Van Loo LLP served as lead counsel to Refract on its sale to Kerberus, announced by Kerberus on August 21, 2025. Refract is the team behind Pocket Universe, a widely adopted browser extension known for clear pre‑transaction simulations and wallet protection, along with a reported ~200,000‑user base. By bringing Pocket Universe together with Kerberus' Sentinel3 security stack. Kerberus has said its roadmap includes keeping the Pocket Universe user experience intact while shipping deeper protections, including broader coverage across EVM chains and Solana and desktop defenses aimed at crypto malware and viruses.
