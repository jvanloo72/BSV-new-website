BSV's tax practice focuses on the tax dimensions of transactions: M&A tax
(including tax-free reorganizations, asset sales, and stock sales),
international and cross-border tax, and the fast-moving area of cryptocurrency
and blockchain taxation. We advise companies, founders, crypto funds, and
blockchain projects on how to structure consideration efficiently and how to
handle the tax treatment of digital assets.

Our lead attorney for this practice is a recognized thought leader on tax
issues for cryptocurrency, has spoken at PLI and other venues, and has
published on the subject. Because tax sits inside almost every transaction, the
tax team works alongside the M&A and IP attorneys so the structure and the
deal stay in step.
