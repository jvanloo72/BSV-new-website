BSV's M&A practice represents public and private acquirers, private-equity
firms, and venture-backed companies and their investors in technology and
life-sciences transactions — both domestic and cross-border. We handle the
work that sits at the heart of a deal: structuring, due diligence, negotiating
the purchase agreement and ancillary documents, and coordinating the tax,
intellectual-property, and regulatory pieces so nothing falls through the
cracks.

Because the team is small and senior, the partner you meet is the partner who
does your deal. When a transaction calls for outside help — antitrust counsel,
for example — we assemble the right team around it rather than over-claiming
what we do in house.
